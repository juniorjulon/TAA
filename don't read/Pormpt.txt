TAA Horizon: Short to Medium Term
Treat this TAA system as if you are auditing it for institutional capital allocation.
Assume that weak logic, poor proxies, or unstable signals will lead to capital misallocation.

Your job is to:

Identify flawed assumptions
Detect hidden risks (regime breaks, collinearity, lagging indicators)
Challenge whether each signal truly adds predictive power

If a metric is weak, say it clearly and propose a better one.
Do not defend the system—stress test it.


You will be given screenshots of a Tactical Asset Allocation (TAA) framework, including metrics, transformations, and economic rationale.

Your task is to critically evaluate and challenge the system, not to describe it.

For each metric or signal shown:

1. Economic validity: Does the rationale hold under different macro regimes (inflationary, recessionary, liquidity-driven)? Is the relationship causal, leading, or just coincidental?

2. Metric quality & construction: Is the chosen series the best proxy? Are there better alternatives used in institutional practice? Identify biases, lagging behavior, or structural weaknesses.

3. Transformation critique: Evaluate the transformation used (z-score, EWMA, rolling, etc.). Is it appropriate for the signal’s nature? Does it introduce instability, noise, or regime dependency?

4. Signal contribution: Is this signal redundant with others? Does it add unique information or just reinforce existing exposure?

5. Implementation risks: Data issues (frequency mismatch, revisions, availability), Overfitting or excessive complexity, Execution relevance (can this actually be traded?)

6. Suggested improvements (mandatory): Refine the existing metric OR propose a better transformation. Suggest alternative series if clearly superior (optional but encouraged). Keep suggestions aligned with real-world asset management practices
